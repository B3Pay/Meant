The interface_id a pack is checked against is the identity of its own fragment: the validator compiles binds.requires (or reads binds.abi, with the struct types of binds.eip712) and resolves every annotation against it. It is not the identity of any ledger or token. A ledger that matches has its own interface_id, and the pack is applied to the ledger's types. A contract profile is checked against its own binds in the same way; the seven shipped here bind EIP-712 types and no function.

How a pack matches

A Candid pack applies to a canister whose service is a subtype of the fragment in binds.requires, with result variants allowed to be wider (D-019); an EVM pack applies to a contract that has every function of its binds.abi with the same selector, output types and kind of call, with no subtyping. A matched pack is applied to the service's own types, so a position the pack names that the service lacks is skipped (I_PACK_ANNOTATION_SKIPPED); the full rules are on Profiles, layers and binding.

An EVM pack may also carry EIP-712 messages (binds.eip712, spec §15.9). It still matches by its binds.abi, and its messages then join the contract's resolved form under messages. The contract profiles (permit2, dai-permit, usdc, usdc-domain-usdc, usdc-base-sepolia, x402-exact-permit2 and x402-upto-permit2) are not packs: no pack search tries them, and they apply when given as profiles (--profile, or profiles in resolveService).

What the packs grant

A grant names who may take tokens because of a call or a signature, how many and until when, as positions of its arguments (Tags). A wallet reads it with the consent functions and checks it against terms the person confirmed for one task (Confirm terms for a task). The token is the one an amount.token names (Permit2's and x402's), else the ledger or contract called. An amount at or above its tag's threshold is unlimited, and an end at or above its tag's threshold never ends. The deadline of EIP-2612's and USDC's Permit and DAI's expiry bound only when the signature may be used, so they are not ends: those allowances never end (spec §5.3). An x402 upto payment grants its maximum: the facilitator chooses the amount it settles, up to it, so terms check the most it can cost. The other transfers and approvals carry no grant: icrc2_transfer_from and ERC-20's transferFrom; and Permit2's PermitBatch and PermitBatchTransferFrom, which grant several tokens while a grant covers one. A request without a grant is not covered by terms and needs its confirmation. A pack whose grant does not hold in the service it is applied to keeps its effect and loses the grant (I_PACK_ANNOTATION_SKIPPED); in the ledger and token runs below every grant holds, and the resolved forms carry them all.

WayWhat it does
--packsTries every pack in the packs directory and applies those that match; the others get I_PACK_NOT_MATCHED (info). A pack for the other kind of interface is skipped: the EVM packs are never tried on a .did, nor the ICRC packs on an ABI. The contract profiles in the same directory are not packs and are never tried.
--packs-dir <dir>The packs directory (default: the standard packs the package ships).
--profile pack.jsonApplies the pack as given: it must match, or the resolve fails with E_PACK_NOT_MATCHED. A contract profile, such as permit2.profile.json, is applied this way too.
loadPacks(dir?)Every *.profile.json of kind "pack" in a directory, sorted by file name; by default DEFAULT_PACKS_DIR, the shipped packs. Packs only: erc20, erc2612, icrc1 and icrc2, never the seven contract profiles. Node entry.
STANDARD_PACKSThe same four packs as data, from @meantjs/profile/browser, for a bundle that cannot read files.
@meantjs/profile/packs/*The eleven files themselves, as a package export (./packs/* maps to the package's data/packs/*): the four packs, and the seven contract profiles, such as @meantjs/profile/packs/permit2.profile.json and @meantjs/profile/packs/x402-upto-permit2.profile.json.

import { readFileSync } from "node:fs";

packs.map((p) => p.doc.name); // ["erc20", "erc2612", "icrc1", "icrc2"]
const r = await resolveService({
  did: readFileSync("ledger.did", "utf8"),
  validateSchema,
  packs: packs.map(({ doc }) => ({ doc })), // tried; applied only if they match
});

Amounts are in 6 decimals, USDC; the verifying contract is the token. fact x402's exact scheme pays by TransferWithAuthorization by default, and its payments through Permit2 may come with a Permit for Permit2 (coinbase/x402 at 0e6e8c5, specs/schemes/exact/scheme_exact_evm.md and specs/extensions/eip2612_gas_sponsoring.md, as spec §15.9 records).

usdc is bound to the domain USD Coin version 2 and to USDC on Ethereum (1), Polygon (137), Base (8453), Arbitrum One (42161) and Avalanche C-Chain (43114), and on the test networks Avalanche Fuji (43113) and Arbitrum Sepolia (421614). usdc-domain-usdc is bound to the domain USDC version 2 and to USDC on XDC (50), Monad (143), Sei (1329) and Celo (42220), and on the test networks XDC Apothem (51), Sei testnet (1328), Monad testnet (10143), Polygon Amoy (80002), Base Sepolia (84532), Arc testnet (5042002) and Celo Sepolia (11142220). usdc-base-sepolia is bound to the domain USDC version 2 and to 0x036C…CF7e on chain 84532. A look-alike token gets none of these words (E_DEPLOYMENT). fact (read on chain, chains 1, 8453, 42161 and 84532 on 2026-10-30, the others on 2026-11-01) each contract's DOMAIN_SEPARATOR(), decimals() and four type hashes. fact (EIP-3009; Circle's EIP3009.sol at fc85788, as spec §15.9 records) a signature is valid only after validAfter and before validBefore, both strictly, so the text reads "Valid after" and "Expires", and only the payee may submit a ReceiveWithAuthorization. CancelAuthorization grants nothing, so no terms cover it and it needs its own confirmation. Permit grants its value to spender with no end, as the EIP-2612 pack reads it: its deadline bounds only when the signature may be submitted, and the allowance lasts until it is spent or replaced (spec §5.3). A value of 2256 − 1 reads Unlimited, and a deadline of 2256 − 1 reads Never.
